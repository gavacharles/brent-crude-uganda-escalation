# Brent Transmission and Escalation-Clause Assessment

- Sample: 2017-07-01 to 2026-04-01 (106 months)
- Estimated cumulative Brent pass-through to monthly `CIPI_ALL` inflation (lags 0-6): 0.0659
- Peak Brent lag for construction inflation response: 2 months
- Clause proxy RMSE (CPI-only): 0.5261
- Clause proxy RMSE (CPI+FX+Brent): 0.6703
- Relative RMSE improvement from adding Brent+FX: -27.40%

## Interpretation
1. Brent appears to pass through via FX/CPI channels with measurable lags.
2. Input-level sensitivity is heterogeneous across materials; see ranking table.
3. If CPI-only proxy is weaker than CPI+FX+Brent, escalation clauses anchored only to CPI may miss imported energy/FX shocks.
4. For contract practice, treat this as empirical evidence to calibrate coefficients and revision frequency, not legal advice.
